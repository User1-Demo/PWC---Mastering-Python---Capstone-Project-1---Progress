# Health Claims Processing Guideline

## Reimbursement claims

Health reimbursement submissions should include an itemized provider invoice
and evidence of service. The claim is reviewed against the covered benefits,
waiting periods, exclusions, and limits in the member's active policy.

## Emergency treatment

Emergency treatment is reviewed under the applicable policy terms. Record the
service date and the documents received; do not promise reimbursement before
the coverage review is complete.
