# Motor Claims Processing Guideline

## Collision claims

Motor collision claims should include the incident date, a description of the
damage, and available repair estimates. The adjuster records the review outcome
and supporting notes in the claim history. Coverage remains subject to the
individual policy schedule, exclusions, and deductible.

## Theft claims

Theft claims should include a police report reference and an inventory of the
reported items. Do not represent an item as covered unless the active policy
wording confirms that coverage.
