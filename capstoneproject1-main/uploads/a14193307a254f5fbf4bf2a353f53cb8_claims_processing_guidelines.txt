Title: Claims Processing Guidelines

Section 1: Claim Workflow
Standard workflow is Claim Submitted, Validation, Assigned to Adjuster, Under Review, Decision as Approved or Rejected, and finally Closed after completion of processing steps.

Section 2: Validation Checks
At submission stage, mandatory fields must be checked including policy linkage, claimant details, claim type, region, amount, incident date, and description. Claims missing required data must not proceed until corrected.

Section 3: Policy Validation
Every claim must be linked to a valid policy. Claims against inactive, cancelled, expired, or otherwise invalid policies must be rejected or returned for clarification according to business rules.

Section 4: Type Matching
Claim type must align with the related policy type. For example, a motor claim must be linked to a motor policy and a health claim must be linked to a health policy.

Section 5: Status Progression
Claims should move forward only through defined states: Submitted, Under Review, Approved or Rejected, and Closed. Direct backward or skipped transitions are not allowed without manager override and recorded justification.

Section 6: Manager Override
A Claims Manager may override a status transition where justified by business urgency, exceptional circumstances, or corrected operational error. Every override must include a written reason and must be audit logged.

Section 7: Adjuster Responsibilities
Adjusters review assigned claims, verify documents, add notes, assess admissibility, and recommend approval or rejection in line with policy wording and internal guidelines.

Section 8: Support Agent Responsibilities
Support agents may check claim status and use approved knowledge sources for customer guidance. Support agents must not approve, reject, or directly alter claim status beyond allowed permissions.

Section 9: Approval Guidance
Approval should be considered only when policy coverage is valid, mandatory documents are reasonably available, facts are consistent, exclusions do not apply, and loss appears admissible.

Section 10: Rejection Guidance
Claims may be rejected for inactive policy, claim outside coverage period, excluded event, insufficient documentation after due follow-up, fraudulent indicators, or policy condition breach.

Section 11: Audit Logging
Claim creation, status change, note addition, document upload, AI query activity, and user management actions must be recorded in audit logs with user and timestamp.

Section 12: Data Protection
Sensitive personal identifiers must not be exposed in logs or operational notes unless explicitly required and protected according to internal security standards.
