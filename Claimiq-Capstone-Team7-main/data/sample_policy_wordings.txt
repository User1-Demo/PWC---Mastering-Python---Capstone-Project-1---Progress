MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY WORDING
Meridian General Insurance

SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW
This policy provides comprehensive coverage for motor vehicles including own-damage
protection and third-party liability. The sum insured is based on the Insured
Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle at the time of policy issuance.

SECTION 2: DEDUCTIBLES
A compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 applies to all own-damage claims for private
cars. For windshield and glass damage claims specifically, a reduced deductible of
INR 500 applies. Commercial vehicles carry a higher compulsory deductible of
INR 2,000.

SECTION 3: WATER AND FLOOD DAMAGE
Flood and water damage to the vehicle, including engine damage caused by water
ingestion, is covered under this policy up to the full Insured Declared Value,
subject to a deductible of 5 percent of the claim amount. Claims arising from
water damage must be supported by evidence of the flooding event, such as news
reports or municipal records.

SECTION 4: THEFT COVERAGE
Total theft of the insured vehicle is covered up to 100 percent of the Insured
Declared Value, provided an FIR is filed with police within 48 hours of discovery
and a copy is submitted along with the claim.

SECTION 5: CLAIM SUBMISSION TIMELINE
All claims must be reported to Meridian General Insurance within 48 hours of the
incident. Claims reported after 7 days without reasonable justification may be
rejected under policy condition 14.2.

SECTION 6: EXCLUSIONS
This policy does not cover damage caused by driving under the influence of alcohol
or drugs, damage occurring while the vehicle is used for purposes other than those
declared at policy inception, or normal wear and tear.

SECTION 7: NO CLAIM BONUS
Policyholders who do not file any claim during the policy period are eligible for
a No Claim Bonus (NCB) discount ranging from 20 percent to 50 percent on renewal,
depending on the number of consecutive claim-free years.
